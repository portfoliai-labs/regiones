OMI

Italy's real estate market data by zone (OMI quotations, zones, perimeters, sales), one file per municipality.

Source: «Agenzia delle Entrate – OMI», licensed under CC BY 4.0 (Forniture dati OMI); this dataset is CC BY 4.0 too. It is not published nor endorsed by the Agenzia delle Entrate. Changes: rearranged by municipality, perimeters simplified within about 3 m; see the manifest.

Start from v1/manifest.json.

Issuers

The bonds of states, regions and local authorities, and international bodies, by ISIN, one file per ISIN prefix. Sources: ESMA FIRDS (reproduction authorised provided the source is acknowledged: ESMA's legal notice) and GLEIF (LEI data, CC0 1.0); this dataset is CC BY 4.0. It is not published nor endorsed by ESMA or GLEIF. Start from v1/issuers/manifest.json.

ISTAT's municipalities

Italy's municipalities by cadastral code: their names, provinces and regions, one file. Source: «ISTAT» (Codici dei comuni, delle province e delle regioni), licensed under CC BY 4.0 (ISTAT's legal notes); this dataset is CC BY 4.0 too. It is not published nor endorsed by ISTAT. Start from v1/istat/manifest.json.
